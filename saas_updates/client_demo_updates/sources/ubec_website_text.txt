874865	UBEC Trains Education Secretaries on School Effectiveness
875081	In a bid to improve school effectiveness and familiarize Education Secretaries with their roles on smooth implementation of the UBE programme, the Commission conducted a 3-day workshop across the 6 Geopolitical Zones on Improving School Effectiveness.
875361	The aim of the training was to expose Education Secretaries to important components of the UBE programme as well as the School Based Teacher Professional Development Model. A team of dedicated UBEC staff (Teacher Development) were deployed to the Six (6) geopolitical zones to coordinate and monitor the training and to also provide support to resource persons and quality assurance officers assigned for effective and efficient conduct of the 3-day training.
875849	The training was conducted in 6 centres in the 6 Geopolitical Zones on 22 – 24 September 2021 (for South South – (Akwaibom), South East (Enugu), South West (Ibadan) and North Central (Nasarawa) zones) and on 27 – 29 September 2021 (for North East (Bauchi) and North West (Kaduna)). Facilitators for the training were experts drawn from similar trainings recently concluded by ESSPIN and Teacher Development Programme of the UKAID. Officers from the Quality Assurance Department of the Commission also sensitized the Education Secretaries on quality assurance methods.
876445	The 3-day workshop included a flag-off ceremony held in Lafia, Nasarawa State. The Flag-off ceremony had in attendance the Executive Governor of Nasarawa State, Governor Abdullahi Sule, the Chairman, Governing Board Universal Basic Education Commission (UBEC) Prof. Adamu Kyuka Usman, the Executive Secretary, UBEC, Dr. Hamid Bobboyi, among others.
876822	The UBEC Board Chairman stressed the need for education secretaries to implement knowledge and skills gained at the workshop for school improvement and the provision of quality education to the Nigerian children as an enduring legacy for the future generation.
877111	In the same vein, the Executive Secretary reiterated that the ultimate goal of the basic education programme is ensuring that all children of school age, irrespective of background, attain a minimum of ten years of quality basic education. He emphasized the relevance of education secretaries in the implementation of basic education programme and the efforts of the Commission at improving the quality of education through capacity building of teachers in the 36 States and FCT from 2015 till date.
877639	He further stressed the challenge of delay in accessing and implementation of TPD funds by the States and the need for measures to be taken in facilitating this process.
877837	In closing, the Executive Secretary pleaded with all stakeholders to join the Commission in ensuring the delivery of quality education in Nigeria.

885115	UBEC Develops Training Manual for History Teachers in Basic Education Schools
885351	As part of concerted efforts to ensure the reintroduction of History into the Basic Education Curriculum, the Commission has developed a training manual for History Teachers to redefine the teaching and learning of History in Basic Education Schools across the 36 states and the FCT.
885663	Following the reintroduction of History into the basic education curriculum, the Commission was saddled with the responsibility of ensuring effective implementation of this mandate, in achieving this, a stakeholders Meeting was held to discuss the reintroduction of History in the basic education curriculum. Based on the recommendations from the meeting, a writer’s workshop was conducted for the development of a manual for training and retraining of History Teachers in basic schools.
886179	The manual is a product of collective intellectual ingenuity and erudition of academics, historians and educational consultants drawn from reputable tertiary institutions in Nigeria, the Historical Society of Nigeria (HSN), organized private sector and UBEC. The manual incorporates modern pedagogy and learner-centred approaches for effective and efficient teaching of History. It included a range of beyond-the-classroom activities such as field-trips, story-telling, drama among others that will reinforce the delivery of the History curriculum with a view to making the teaching-learning experience fun, practical and permanent.
886840	Meanwhile, the Commission is already making plans to disseminate the content of the manual through a Step down training which includes a Train-the-Trainer workshop, and Cascading workshop across the 36 states and the FCT. The training is expected to acquaint History Teachers and para-historians in basic education schools with the content of the manual for improved and result-oriented delivery of the History curriculum.
887291	The Executive Secretary, Dr Hammid Bobboyi, in his remark believes that the reintroduction of History into the Basic Education Curricular will strengthen national consciousness, patriotism and help the younger generation acquire relevant knowledge of their country and localities, their cultural norms, values, heroes and legends for meaningful contributions to the development of the country.

1153140	The Universal Basic Education Commission (UBEC) is a statutory body established under the
1153265	Compulsory, Free Universal Basic Education and Other Related Matters Act, 2004 (UBE Act, 2004)
1153364	. The Commission is responsible for coordinating, regulating, monitoring, and supporting the effective implementation of the Universal Basic Education programme across Nigeria.
1153572	The UBE Act provides for
1153632	nine years of compulsory and free basic education
1153686	, comprising six years of primary education and three years of junior secondary education, as a fundamental right for children.
1154014	Policy Formulation and Standards
1154075	UBEC formulates policy guidelines for the effective implementation of the Universal Basic Education programme and prescribes minimum standards for basic education nationwide. The Commission also monitors compliance with these standards.
1154341	Funding and Resource Allocation
1154401	The Commission receives federal block grants and allocates funds to States, Local Governments, and other relevant implementing agencies in accordance with approved guidelines and funding formulas. UBEC also monitors the utilisation of funds to ensure that resources are applied for their intended purposes.
1154737	Planning and Development
1154790	UBEC works with States, Local Governments, and other stakeholders to develop periodic plans for the balanced and coordinated development of basic education. The Commission also advises the Federal Government on funding requirements and the orderly development of basic education.
1155099	Curriculum and Quality Assurance
1155160	The Commission supports the development and dissemination of curricula, syllabi, instructional materials, and other learning resources for basic education. It promotes the use of approved national curricula across early childhood care and development centres, primary schools, and junior secondary schools.
1155496	Teacher and Personnel Development
1155558	UBEC collaborates with States and Local Governments to conduct personnel audits and supports capacity-building and professional development programmes for teachers, education managers, and other personnel involved in basic education delivery.
1155830	Data, Research and Policy Support
1155892	The Commission maintains a comprehensive basic education data bank and conducts research to generate evidence that supports effective policy formulation, planning, implementation, and improvement of basic education.
1156137	Partnership and Stakeholder Engagement
1156204	UBEC coordinates basic education activities with non-governmental organisations, development partners, donor agencies, and other stakeholders. The Commission also promotes mass mobilisation and public sensitisation to encourage community participation and ownership of basic education initiatives.
1156531	Monitoring and Accountability
1156589	UBEC monitors the implementation of projects and programmes funded through federal intervention, assesses compliance with approved plans, and promotes transparency and accountability in the utilisation of basic education resources.

1157107	equitable access to quality basic education
1157155	, address educational disparities, strengthen teaching and learning, and support the development of an inclusive and effective basic education system across Nigeria.
